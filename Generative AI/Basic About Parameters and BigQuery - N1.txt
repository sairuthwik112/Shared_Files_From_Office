What is SynthID?
SynthID is a technology developed by Google DeepMind that embeds a digital watermark directly into the pixels of AI-generated images. This watermark is designed to be imperceptible to the human eye but detectable by an algorithm. Its purpose is to help identify images as AI-generated, promoting transparency and responsible AI practices, even if the image is later modified (e.g., compressed, filtered).

Explore Data with Gemini in BigQuery

BigQuery is used to store, process, and analyze very large amounts of data quickly using SQL.
BigQuery helps you analyze huge datasets in seconds—without managing servers.

As a data analyst at Data Beans, you are moving from simple queries to more complex analysis and need to work more independently. By using BigQuery’s Gemini features, you can describe your goals in plain English and let Gemini generate SQL, explain existing queries, and transform results (for example, formatting or adding logic) without rewriting everything manually. When you get stuck, Gemini can review and debug your SQL, suggest fixes, and explain why errors occur. This helps you quickly build confidence, handle complex queries on your own, and deliver deeper insights more efficiently—while still validating results through the Table Explorer and query runs.
